## Foundational OO and design literature

- Bertrand Meyer, *Object-Oriented Software Construction, 2nd ed.* - rigorous OO foundations, contracts, inheritance, genericity. Author resource: https://bertrandmeyer.com/oosc2/
- Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, John Vlissides, *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software* - canonical GoF pattern catalog.
- Grady Booch et al., *Object-Oriented Analysis and Design with Applications* - OO analysis/design and large-system modeling.
- Craig Larman, *Applying UML and Patterns, 3rd ed.* - OO analysis/design, GRASP, iterative development.
- Rebecca Wirfs-Brock, Alan McKean, *Object Design: Roles, Responsibilities, and Collaborations* - responsibility-driven design.
- Sandi Metz, *Practical Object-Oriented Design* - practical dependency/interface/composition reasoning.
- Barbara Liskov, Jeannette Wing, "A Behavioral Notion of Subtyping" - behavioral foundations of substitutability. https://www.cs.cmu.edu/Groups/venari/subtype-toplas.html

## Refactoring, testing, and legacy systems

- Martin Fowler, *Refactoring, 2nd ed.* - behavior-preserving design improvement. https://martinfowler.com/books/refactoring.html
- Steve Freeman, Nat Pryce, *Growing Object-Oriented Software, Guided by Tests* - TDD and object collaboration.
- Michael Feathers, *Working Effectively with Legacy Code* - seams, characterization tests, dependency breaking.
- Kent Beck, *Implementation Patterns* - code-level design and communication of intent.

## Domain and architecture

- Eric Evans, *Domain-Driven Design: Tackling Complexity in the Heart of Software* - domain modeling, bounded contexts, tactical patterns.
- Martin Fowler, *Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture* - enterprise/domain/persistence patterns. https://martinfowler.com/eaaCatalog/
- OMG UML 2.5.1 - current formal UML version in the source snapshot. https://www.omg.org/spec/UML/2.5.1
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022 - architecture description. https://www.iso.org/standard/74393.html

## C++ language and coding guidance

- ISO/IEC 14882:2024, *Programming languages - C++* (C++23 publication). https://www.iso.org/standard/83626.html
- C++ Core Guidelines, living guidance led by Bjarne Stroustrup and Herb Sutter. https://isocpp.github.io/CppCoreGuidelines/
- SEI CERT C++ Coding Standard. https://wiki.sei.cmu.edu/confluence/display/cplusplus/SEI+CERT+C%2B%2B+Coding+Standard

## Software engineering standards

- ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2026, *Software life cycle processes*. https://www.iso.org/standard/90219.html
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023, *System life cycle processes*. https://www.iso.org/standard/81702.html
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018, *Requirements engineering*. Confirmed current in 2024 but replacement work exists; verify before regulated use. https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html
- ISO/IEC 25010:2023, *Product quality model*. https://www.iso.org/standard/78176.html
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 15289:2019, *Content of life-cycle information items*. Confirmed current in 2025, with a successor under development. https://committee.iso.org/standard/74909.html
- IEEE Computer Society, *SWEBOK Guide V4.0a* - consensus software-engineering body of knowledge. https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/resources/

## Regulated profiles

- MISRA C++:2023, *Guidelines for the use of C++:17 in critical systems*. Official announcement: https://misra.org.uk/misra-cpp2023-released-including-hardcopy/
- AUTOSAR, *Guidelines for the use of the C++14 language in critical and safety-related systems*, Release 18-10; document marks itself obsolete. https://www.autosar.org/fileadmin/standards/R18-10-1/AP/AUTOSAR_RS_CPP14Guidelines.pdf
- ISO 26262:2018, *Road vehicles - Functional safety*; Part 6 covers software product development. https://www.iso.org/publication/PUB200262.html
- IEC 61508:2010 Edition 2, functional safety series; verify current edition. https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/22273
- RTCA DO-178C software assurance family. RTCA identifies DO-332 as the OO technology supplement and DO-330 for tool qualification. https://www.rtca.org/do-178/

## Source-use discipline

Use these sources to establish concepts, terminology, decision rules, and applicability. For licensed standards/books, paraphrase and cite rather than reproducing protected text. When a user's organization supplies an authorized copy, inspect the exact clause only as permitted by the host and copyright constraints.
