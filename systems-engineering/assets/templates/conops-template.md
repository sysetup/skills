## Minimal example

Illustrative only; not a record of execution, approval, or measured performance.

- Mission: deliver time-critical medical parcels inside a named urban area without using road vehicles for the last 5 km.
- Gap: ground couriers cannot meet a 20-minute clinical window during peak traffic.
- MOE: percentage of parcels that arrive inside the clinical window under stated weather limits.
- Nominal scenario: hospital dispatcher releases a parcel; the UAV flies a geo-fenced corridor; a receiving nurse confirms custody.
