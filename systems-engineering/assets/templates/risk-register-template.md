## Minimal example

Illustrative only; not a record of execution, approval, or measured performance.

| Risk ID | Risk statement (cause -> event -> effect) | Category | Likelihood | Consequence | Severity | Treatment strategy | Mitigation actions | Owner | Residual risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TR-01 | if single generator power is lost, flight-control computer may fail, causing loss of aircraft control | technical | medium | extreme | high | reduce | add redundant generator and diverse battery backup | chief engineer | projected medium; not demonstrated | open |

Use approved likelihood/consequence scales and identify the source, exposure, and assessment
owner. The example mitigations and residual level are hypotheses, not proof of flight safety.
Record verification evidence and an authorized residual-risk decision before closing an action.
