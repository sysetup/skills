Use states proposed/planned/executed/checked/accepted/superseded. Outcome is independent:
not-run/pass/fail/inconclusive. Never promote a row solely because its columns are filled.
An actual record must be inspected and accessible; retain observations, anomalies, failures,
limitations, reviewer scope, and authorized acceptance decisions. See reference 12.
