## Interview protocol (qualitative; Creswell structure)

- Study title
- Design (phenomenology / narrative / grounded theory / ethnography / case / nested qual in mixed)
- Central phenomenon
- Participant code (not a real name in drafts)
- Date, place, duration, recorder
- Consent reminder (purpose, voluntary, withdrawal, confidentiality, distress stop-rule)
- Bracketing note (researcher, 5–8 lines) attached, not read to the participant

Icebreaker (1)
- Tell me about your current work / situation in {context}.

Central questions (4–7). Phenomenology uses these two first:
1. What have you experienced in terms of {phenomenon}?
2. What contexts or situations have typically influenced or affected your experience of {phenomenon}?
3. {subquestion}
4. {subquestion}

Probes: "What was that like?", "Can you walk me through a specific moment?", "What else?"

Closing
- What else should I have asked about {phenomenon}?
- Permission to follow up; member-check offer.

Field log after the interview: setting, nonverbal, interruptions, researcher reactions.

## Experiment session script (experimental family)

1. Eligibility and consent
2. Pretest measure (if the design includes it) — instrument ID
3. Random assignment record (or quasi assignment rule)
4. Treatment delivery — fidelity checklist
5. Contamination reminder / usage log if the treatment is a shareable tool
6. Posttest measure — instrument ID
7. Debrief
