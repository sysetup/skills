# Consistency matrix

Alignment artifact for Creswell's purpose–question–methods consistency. Not a Creswell chapter title. Copy the table into the protocol. One row per research question or hypothesis. Empty cells fail the quality gate.

Delete unused columns only when the approach makes them impossible (e.g. no hypotheses in phenomenology). Do not leave instructional text in the user-facing table.
